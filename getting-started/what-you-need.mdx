## A crypto wallet

Kimia signs you in with a wallet you already have. Phantom, MetaMask and Solflare all
work.

You do not need to fund this wallet directly. It is used to prove who you are and to
approve Kimia once. The money goes elsewhere, which the next pages cover.

## Funds on the right network

This is the part that catches people out.

Kimia trades on two exchanges that live on two different blockchains, and nothing
moves money between them.

| Exchange | Runs on | You need |
|---|---|---|
| Hyperliquid | Its own chain, funded from Ethereum or Arbitrum | USDC |
| Pacifica | Solana | USDC on Solana |

If you want to use both, you fund both. Separately. Money in your Hyperliquid account
cannot open a position on Pacifica, and the reverse is also true.

## A sensible starting amount

There is a floor. Each side of a position must be worth at least 10 USD, so the
practical minimum is around 25 to 30 USD once you account for both sides and the
buffer that keeps the short position safe.

That is the technical minimum, not a recommendation. Fees are roughly fixed per
transaction, so at very small sizes they consume a large share of what you earn. A
few hundred dollars is a more realistic place to start seeing the yield clearly.

## About twenty minutes

Most of it is waiting for deposits to confirm. The Kimia side takes a couple of
minutes.

<Note>
You will be asked to sign one message per exchange during setup. That signature grants
a trading permission. It never moves money, and it is the only signature Kimia asks
for. Everything after that happens without interrupting you.
</Note>

## What you do not need

You do not need to understand perpetual futures in detail. You do not need to watch
charts or time an entry. You do not need to keep a browser tab open, since positions
stay open at the exchange whether or not you are looking at them.
