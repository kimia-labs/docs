<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Where does the yield actually come from">
A fee called the funding rate, paid hourly by leveraged traders on a perpetual futures
exchange. It is not newly issued tokens, not a share of protocol revenue, and not an
incentive programme. Somebody is genuinely paying it. See
[How it works](/how-it-works).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is the return guaranteed">
No. The funding rate changes hourly and can be negative, meaning the position costs
you money rather than paying you. Any annualized figure describes the present moment,
not the future.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get in">
Kimia is in private beta. You need an invite code from an existing user, or a place
from the waitlist. See [Invite codes](/getting-started/invite-code).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Kimia take my money">
No. Your funds sit in your own master wallets and exchange accounts. The agent's
permission covers trading only, and the exchange enforces that limit. It cannot
withdraw or transfer. See [Your money](/safety/your-money).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why are there wallets I did not create">
When you sign in, Privy creates an Ethereum master and a Solana master for you. The
exchanges need those kinds of address, and they hold your money. They are yours, and
you can export their keys from the account menu at any time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How often will I be asked to sign">
Once per exchange to bind the agent, and once per exchange to approve Kimia's fee.
After that, trades on Hyperliquid and Pacifica need no signature. Trades that use
Jupiter need one signature each, because Solana has no way to delegate trading.
Deposits need their own signatures.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Kimia charge a fee">
Yes. 0.05 percent of the short side's size when it opens, and again when it closes.
Nothing on the spot side. Exchange, bridge and network fees are separate. See
[What it costs](/using-kimia/what-it-costs).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I lose money">
Yes. Funding can turn negative, the leveraged short side can be liquidated, an
exchange can auto deleverage your position, fees can exceed what you earned, and an
exchange itself can fail. [Risks](/safety/risks) covers each in detail.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the minimum">
Each side of a position must be worth at least 10 USD: 20 USD in total at 1x, 15 USD
at 2x. That is the technical minimum. The bridge fee is flat, so small deposits lose a
large share to costs. A few hundred dollars is a more realistic start.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long should I hold">
Long enough for the funding collected to exceed the cost of opening and closing.
Weeks rather than days. Frequent trading is the most common way people lose money on
this strategy.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why do I need to fund exchanges separately">
Hyperliquid runs on its own chain and Pacifica and Jupiter run on Solana. Money on one
cannot open a position on another. The in-app deposit splits your USDC between
Solana and Hyperliquid in one go, but after that nothing moves money between them
automatically. If you use only Hyperliquid, you only fund Hyperliquid.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="One side of my position shows a big loss">
Look at both together. If the long side shows a matching gain, the hedge is doing its
job. Neither number means anything on its own.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if Kimia goes offline">
Your position stays open at the exchange and keeps accruing funding. You can close it
through the exchange's own website at any time, without Kimia, using your exported
master wallet key. Export it before you need it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why was my order refused">
Kimia checks orders before placing them and refuses rather than proceeding when
something is outside its limits. A refusal is the system working.
[Troubleshooting](/safety/troubleshooting) lists the common reasons.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do referrals work">
Share your invite link. Once your referrals have traded 5,000 USD and you have traded
10,000 USD, you earn 5 to 35 percent of the Kimia fees they generate, depending on
your tier. See [Referrals](/using-kimia/referrals).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a token or an airdrop">
These docs cover using the product. Nothing here should be read as implying a
distribution of any kind. Points shown on the leaderboard are not active.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I stop">
Close your positions, export your master wallet keys, revoke the agent on each
exchange you bound, and withdraw. None of these steps require Kimia to cooperate.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I contact support">
Email [kimia.support@gmail.com](mailto:kimia.support@gmail.com). Never send a
private key to anyone, including us.
</Accordion>
