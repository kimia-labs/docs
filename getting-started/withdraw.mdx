title: "Withdraw"
description: "Getting USDC back to your own wallet."
---

Withdrawals go to one place only: the Solana wallet you signed in with. There is no
option to send to another address.

## The Withdraw tab

On the **Wallet** page, open **Withdraw**. Enter how much to take from each source:

| Box | Takes from |
|---|---|
| **From your Solana master** | USDC in your Solana master |
| **From HyperCore** | USDC in your Hyperliquid account. While a position is open this reads **From HyperCore spot** |

Press **Withdraw**. The app shows each leg as it goes.

## From your Solana master

A direct transfer on Solana. Your sign-in wallet asks you to approve once and pays the
small network fee. It lands in seconds.

## From HyperCore

Kimia signs the withdrawal from your Ethereum master for you, without a prompt, and
Circle bridges it to Solana. It usually lands within a few minutes.

| | |
|---|---|
| Minimum | 2 USD. Circle's fee comes out of it |
| Held back | 0.50 USD stays behind for Hyperliquid's withdrawal gas, which it charges in USDC |
| While a position is open | Only spot balance can be withdrawn. Perp margin stays put |

If it takes longer than expected, the app says so. Check again shortly before
retrying.

## Money in the Kimia vault

The Withdraw tab does not touch the vault. On the **Vault** tab:

- **Withdraw** moves deposited USDC back to your Solana master
- **Withdraw as USDC** sells a token and returns the USDC
- **Withdraw as** a token takes the token out unsold

Then withdraw from your Solana master as above. See
[The Kimia vault](/using-kimia/kimia-vault).

## Before you withdraw everything

Close open positions first. Funds backing a live position are not available.

## Without Kimia

If Kimia is unavailable, export your master wallet keys from the account menu with
**Export key**, import them into a wallet you control, and withdraw directly from
Hyperliquid or on-chain. The vault program also lets your Solana master withdraw
without Kimia. See [Your money](/safety/your-money#getting-your-keys).
