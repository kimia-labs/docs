## This may suit you if

You already hold stablecoins or crypto that is sitting idle, and you want it to earn
something without taking a directional bet.

You are comfortable with the idea that the return moves around. Some weeks pay well,
some weeks pay nothing, and some weeks cost you money.

You can leave a position open for weeks rather than days. Opening and closing costs
money in fees each time, so quick round trips eat the yield.

You understand that an exchange holding your deposit is a risk in itself, separate
from anything Kimia does.

## This probably does not suit you if

**You need the money back on a fixed date.** Positions can be closed at any time, but
closing during an unfavourable moment means accepting whatever the market gives you.

**You are looking for a guaranteed rate.** There is none. The funding rate is set by
supply and demand for leverage, and it can be negative.

**You want to set it and forget it for a year.** Funding conditions change. Positions
benefit from being checked on.

**You are depositing money you cannot afford to lose.** Kimia is unaudited software
in private beta. The exchanges it trades on carry their own risks. Both statements
are true at once.

## What returns look like

Funding rates on major assets have historically run somewhere in the range of a few
percent to a few tens of percent annualized, with wide swings and periods of
negative rates.

Kimia shows each asset's Carry APR before you open anything: the rate expected over
the next hour, multiplied out to a year, before fees. It refreshes about every 30
seconds. Treat it as a snapshot, not a forecast. It describes what is being paid
right now, not what will be paid over the life of your position.

Some assets on Hyperliquid sit at around 11 percent APR most of the time. That is the
exchange's default rate when longs and shorts are balanced, not a sign of strong
demand.

<Warning>
Be sceptical of any annualized figure, including ours. Annualizing an hourly rate
assumes the rate holds for a year. It rarely does.
</Warning>

## The honest summary

This is a real yield with a real source, which puts it ahead of a lot of things in
crypto. It is also a leveraged position on a young exchange, run by young software,
and that combination has ways of going wrong that a savings account does not.

Size your first position accordingly. See [Risks](/safety/risks) for the specific
failure modes, written plainly.
