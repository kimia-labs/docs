Both pages are in the top navigation, next to **Trading** and **Wallet**.

## Portfolio

The **Portfolio** page summarises your account. Switch between **All**, **Orders**
and **PnL report** at the top.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| PnL | Profit and loss over time |
| Analytics | Wallet balance, average buy amount, average entry and exit market cap, average hold time, biggest win and loss |
| Distribution | How your results are spread across outcomes |

### Your pairs

Below the cards, **Open Pairs** and **Closed Pairs** list your positions pair by pair.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pair, Route | The asset, and which venue holds the spot and the short |
| Size, Spot, Short, Net | As on the [positions list](/using-kimia/your-position) |
| Funding, Earned, uPnL | Rate, funding collected, unrealized result |
| Status | **Close pair** for open pairs, **Closed** for closed ones |

**Filter** narrows the list by name, spot venue, short size (1,000 or 10,000 USD and
up), funding (earning or paying), earned and uPnL (profit or loss), and pairs whose
hedge has drifted. **Close all pairs** closes every open pair after a second
confirmation. See [Closing a position](/using-kimia/closing#closing-everything).

<Note>
Closed pairs are recorded from 28 October 2026. Pairs closed before then do not
appear. A newly closed pair can take a few minutes to show while its fills are
confirmed.
</Note>

### Sharing a pair

The arrow next to a pair's name, here or on the positions list, opens **Share this
pair**. The card shows the pair and its route, the current APR for an open pair or the
funding return for a closed one, entry price, and mark price and funding earned or
close price. It carries your referral code.

Copy the link or post it to X. Anyone opening the link sees the card and a
**Start trading** button that pre-fills your invite code. Links are signed, so an
edited link shows nothing. On a phone, **Hide amounts** removes dollar figures from the
card and the link.

## Leaderboard

The **Leaderboard** page ranks Kimia users. Choose a period: **1 Day**, **1 Week**,
**1 Month** or **All Time**, the default.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Place | Rank for the period |
| User | The start of the wallet address |
| PnL | Funding settled in the period |
| ROI | That funding, divided by current account value |
| Volume | Total value of filled orders in the period |

1 Month means the last 30 days. The top 100 are shown.

Your own standing appears in the overview panel alongside your referral details:
**Referrals**, **Referral Tier** and **Referral Earnings**. **Total Points** is shown
but points are not active.

<Note>
The leaderboard is public. Anyone can see the start of your wallet address and your
figures for each period.
</Note>

For referrals, see [Referrals](/using-kimia/referrals).
