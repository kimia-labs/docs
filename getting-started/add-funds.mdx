title: "Deposit and bridge"
description: "Getting USDC onto each exchange."
---

Your money goes into your own master wallets and your own exchange accounts. Kimia
never holds it.

Open the account menu, top right. It has three tabs: **Wallets**, **Deposit** and
**Bridge**.

## The Deposit tab

This is the easiest route. Enter one amount of USDC and Kimia splits it evenly:

| Half | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Solana | Your Solana master, as USDC on Solana |
| HyperCore | Bridged by Across to USDC on HyperCore, credited to your Ethereum master |

The money comes from the wallet you signed in with, so that wallet needs USDC on
Solana and about 0.01 SOL. Your wallet asks you to sign twice, once for each half.
The bridged half usually arrives within seconds.

<Note>
The HyperCore figure shown before you confirm is what Across says will actually
arrive, after its fee. It is slightly less than half of what you send.
</Note>

## The Bridge tab

Moves USDC on Solana to your Hyperliquid account without the split. Use it to top up
the Hyperliquid side on its own. It asks for one signature.

## Depositing directly

On the **Wallets** tab, each master wallet has a **Deposit** button that shows its
address and a QR code.

- **Ethereum master.** Send only USDC on HyperCore.
- **Solana master.** Send only USDC on Solana.

Sending any other token or network to these addresses risks losing it.

## Bridge fees and limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Across fee | Roughly 1.26 USD flat, whatever the size. The quote is authoritative |
| Hard minimum | Each half must be worth more than the fee, so about 2.52 USD in total |
| Suggested minimum | 20 USD. Below that the app warns you, since each side of a position needs at least 10 USD |
| SOL needed | About 0.01 SOL in your sign-in wallet |

## Where the money lands

**Hyperliquid.** Bridged USDC arrives in your HyperCore spot balance. When you open a
position, Kimia moves whatever the short side needs from spot to perp for you.

**Solana.** USDC lands in your Solana master. That balance buys Jupiter spot directly.

**Pacifica.** A Pacifica short needs USDC deposited into Pacifica itself, not just
sitting in your Solana master. The account menu shows this as **Pacifica equity** and
**Pacifica available**. Until the first deposit it reads "No Pacifica account yet".

<Warning>
Moving USDC from your Solana master into Pacifica is not yet part of the Kimia app.
For now, use **Export key** on the Solana master card, import that key into a Solana
wallet, and deposit at Pacifica directly. Pacifica's minimum deposit is 10 USDC, plus
a little SOL for the transaction.

Binding the agent is not enough on its own. Without a Pacifica deposit, the short side
of your position will be refused and the position will not open.
</Warning>

## Which exchange to fund

If you are starting out, a position with Hyperliquid on both sides is the simplest:
one deposit via the Bridge tab, no extra signatures per trade, and a single account
to watch.

Cross-exchange pairs, such as Jupiter spot with a Hyperliquid short, sometimes pay
better. They also mean keeping enough balance on both sides, since Kimia cannot move
money between exchanges to cover a shortfall.

## How much to leave spare

Do not put every last dollar into a position.

The short side is backed by a margin buffer held against that position alone,
called isolated margin. If the price rises sharply, that buffer absorbs the move. If
it runs out, the exchange closes the position at a loss, which is called
liquidation, and your hedge disappears when you most need it.

Spare balance on the exchange does not protect the short automatically. It gives you
funds to add to the position's margin yourself if needed. See [Risks](/safety/risks).

## Withdrawing

Kimia has no withdraw button. You withdraw through the exchange, or from the master
wallet on-chain, and Kimia cannot block it.

Because the master wallets were created for you by Privy, you first need their keys.
Use **Export key** on the relevant card in the account menu, import the key into a
wallet you control, then withdraw as normal.

Close any open position first. Funds backing a live position are not available to
withdraw.
