<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Where does the yield actually come from">
A fee called the funding rate, paid hourly by leveraged traders on Hyperliquid's
perpetual futures. It is not newly issued tokens, not a share of protocol revenue, and
not an incentive programme. Somebody is genuinely paying it. See
[How it works](/how-it-works).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is the return guaranteed">
No. The funding rate changes hourly and can be negative, meaning the position costs
you money rather than paying you. Any annualized figure describes the present moment,
not the future.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get in">
Kimia is in private beta at [beta.kimia.live](https://beta.kimia.live). You need an
invite code from an existing user, or a place from the waitlist. See
[Invite codes](/getting-started/invite-code).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Kimia take my money">
No. On Hyperliquid, Kimia's agent can trade but not withdraw. In the Kimia vault,
Kimia can buy within limits you sign, sell, and return funds to you, but the vault
program refuses to send them anywhere else. The honest caveat: Kimia can sell vault
tokens without asking, at whatever price the market gives. See
[Your money](/safety/your-money).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the Kimia vault">
A program on Solana, run by Kimia, that holds spot bought through Kimia, with your
balance recorded against your Solana wallet. It lets Kimia close your position without
asking you to sign. See
[The Kimia vault](/using-kimia/kimia-vault).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should I use Hyperliquid or Kimia for spot">
Kimia marks whichever it recommends for each asset. Hyperliquid spot needs no extra
setup or signatures. Kimia spot needs a little SOL and one signature per buy, and
sometimes buys cheaper. Both pair with the same Hyperliquid short.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why are there wallets I did not create">
When you sign in, Privy creates an Ethereum master and a Solana master for you. They
hold your money. They are yours, and you can export their keys from the account menu at
any time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How often will I be asked to sign">
Once to bind the Hyperliquid agent. Once per deposit. Once per Kimia spot buy,
sometimes twice on a token's first. Closing never needs a signature. Kimia's fee
approval will be one more, once, when it arrives in the app.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Kimia charge a fee">
Yes. 0.05 percent of the short's size when it opens, and again when it closes. Nothing
on the spot side. Exchange, bridge and network fees are separate. See
[What it costs](/using-kimia/what-it-costs).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I lose money">
Yes. Funding can turn negative, the short can be liquidated, Hyperliquid can auto
deleverage it, fees can exceed what you earned, and the exchange or the vault program
can fail. [Risks](/safety/risks) covers each in detail.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the minimum">
Each side of a position must be worth at least 11 USD: 22 USD in total at 1x, 16.50
USD at 2x. That is the technical minimum. A few hundred dollars is a more realistic
start, since fees eat most of the yield on small positions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long should I hold">
Long enough for the funding collected to exceed the cost of opening and closing.
Weeks rather than days. Frequent trading is the most common way people lose money on
this strategy.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get money in and out">
The Wallet page. Deposit sends USDC from your Solana wallet to Hyperliquid and your
Solana master in one transaction, with amounts you choose. Withdraw sends it back to
the same wallet. See [Deposit and bridge](/getting-started/add-funds) and
[Withdraw](/getting-started/withdraw).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="One side of my position shows a big loss">
Look at both together. If the other side shows a matching gain, the hedge is doing its
job. Neither number means anything on its own.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if my short is liquidated">
You lose the short's margin. Kimia then sells your spot automatically so you are not
left unhedged. See [Risks](/safety/risks#liquidation-on-the-short-side).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if Kimia goes offline">
Your position stays open and keeps accruing funding. You can close the short at
Hyperliquid and withdraw from your vault directly, using your exported master wallet
keys. Export them before you need them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why was my order refused">
Kimia checks orders before placing them and refuses rather than proceeding when
something is outside its limits. A refusal is the system working.
[Troubleshooting](/safety/troubleshooting) lists the common reasons.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use Kimia on my phone">
Yes, in your phone's browser at [beta.kimia.live](https://beta.kimia.live). See
[Kimia on your phone](/using-kimia/mobile).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I have a position on Pacifica">
Kimia no longer opens positions on Pacifica, but existing ones still show in your
positions list and **Close** still works. You can also manage them at Pacifica with
your exported Solana master key.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do referrals work">
Share your invite link. Once your referrals have traded 5,000 USD and you have traded
10,000 USD, you earn 5 to 35 percent of the Kimia fees they generate, depending on
your tier. See [Referrals](/using-kimia/referrals).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a token">
Yes. $KIMIA is a token on Solana, raised through a futarchy sale on MetaDAO. Using the
Kimia app does not require holding it, and these docs do not cover it. Nothing here is
an offer of it or a statement about its value. Leaderboard points are not active.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I stop">
Close your positions, withdraw everything, revoke your vault authorizations on the
Vault tab, and revoke the Hyperliquid agent at Hyperliquid. Export your keys first so
you keep access throughout.
</Accordion>

</Accordion>
