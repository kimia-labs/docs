---
title: "Closing a position"
description: "Getting your money back out."
---

Press **Close** on the position's row. Kimia unwinds both sides for you.

## What happens

On Hyperliquid and Pacifica, the short closes first, then the asset is sold. No
signing prompt appears.

That order is deliberate. If the second step fails, you are left holding the asset,
which you can sell at any time. The reverse order could leave you holding only a
short position, which is exposed to the price rising.

**If Jupiter holds the spot side**, the order flips. Your Solana master signs the
Jupiter sale first, then Kimia closes the short. If you reject the signing prompt,
nothing closes.

**On Hyperliquid**, margin released from the closed short is swept back to your spot
balance automatically. Hyperliquid's own balances screen shows spot and perp
separately, so look at spot.

## Selling the spot side on its own

The **Spot Holdings** tab has a **Sell** button on each holding. It sells the spot
side only.

Be clear about what that means when the holding is part of an open pair: selling it
removes the hedge. What remains is a short position, which loses money if the price
rises. If you do this by accident, close the pair too.

There is no button to close only the short. Use **Close** on the pair.

## The dust that gets left behind

You will usually be left with a very small amount of the asset, worth a fraction of a
dollar. It shows under **Spot Holdings**.

The reason is that exchanges charge spot fees in the asset you receive, and they only
accept order sizes in fixed increments. Buy a round number, pay the fee in the asset,
and the amount you now hold is no longer a round number. Selling all of it is
impossible, so a remainder stays behind.

On Hyperliquid, dust under one dollar is converted automatically once a day, at 00:00
UTC.

Kimia refuses to open positions on assets where this remainder would be larger than
one dollar. You will see a refusal mentioning residue or dust.

## How long it takes

Both sides close within seconds under normal conditions. The funds return to your
exchange account or master wallet, not to Kimia.

From there you can open a new position, or withdraw. See
[Deposit and bridge](/getting-started/add-funds#withdrawing).

## If a close fails partway

Check both tabs. Whatever is still open is still open, and you can close it again.

If the short closed but the asset is still showing under **Spot Holdings**, sell it
with **Sell**. If the asset sold but the short is still open, close it promptly. You
are unhedged until you do.

Closing still works when market data is stale, and when opening has been paused.

If closing keeps failing, see [Troubleshooting](/safety/troubleshooting). You can also
close positions directly at the exchange, which does not depend on Kimia working.
That needs your master wallet's key, from **Export key** in the account menu.
