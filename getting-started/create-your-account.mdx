<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with your wallet">
    Open the app and connect Phantom, MetaMask or Solflare.

    Kimia creates two accounts for you behind the scenes, one on Ethereum and one on
    Solana, because the two exchanges need different kinds of address. You will see
    both listed in your account. They belong to you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve trading on an exchange">
    Pick Hyperliquid or Pacifica and approve it. Your wallet will ask you to sign a
    message.

    Read it before you sign. It says you are authorising a second address to trade on
    your behalf. It does not authorise moving or withdrawing funds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repeat for the second exchange, if you want both">
    Each exchange needs its own approval. The signature is different on each because
    the two chains work differently.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you just approved

Kimia creates a separate address called an **agent**. The approval tells the exchange
that this agent is allowed to place and cancel orders on your account.

What the agent can do:

- Open and close positions
- Cancel orders
- Change the leverage on your account

What the agent cannot do:

- Withdraw your funds
- Move money to another address
- Approve anything else on your behalf

This is why the signature is worth reading rather than clicking through. The
distinction between trading permission and withdrawal permission is the whole
security model, and it is enforced by the exchange, not by Kimia.

You can revoke it at any time. See [Your money](/safety/your-money).

## One signature, once

After this approval you will not be asked to sign again. Opening and closing
positions happens without a wallet prompt.

Some people find this surprising. It is the point of the agent: without it, every
adjustment would need you present and signing.

## If Hyperliquid rejects the approval

Hyperliquid requires your account to hold some funds before it will accept an agent
approval. If you have not deposited yet, the approval fails with a message about the
account being unfunded.

Deposit first, then approve. The Pacifica side does not have this requirement.
