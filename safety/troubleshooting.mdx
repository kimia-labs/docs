## I cannot get past the invite screen

See [Invite codes](/getting-started/invite-code#if-your-code-is-refused) for each
message and what it means.

## Binding the agent failed

**On Hyperliquid, with a message about an unfunded account.** The exchange requires a
balance before it accepts an agent. Deposit first, then bind. See
[Deposit and bridge](/getting-started/add-funds).

**With a message that the wallet is already approved.** It worked already, possibly on
an earlier attempt. Nothing further is needed.

**With a message that the address is already bound to another account.** That master
wallet is linked to a different Kimia account. Sign in with the wallet you used
originally.

**With a message about the master address.** Your exchange account is bound to a
different wallet than the one you are signed in with. Sign in with the original
wallet.

## My deposit did not arrive

The bridged half usually lands within seconds. Check the **Wallets** tab in the
account menu for the Ethereum master's spot balance.

- **Refused before signing.** The amount is too small to cover the bridge fee. Each
  half must be worth more than about 1.26 USD
- **Failed while signing.** Check your sign-in wallet holds about 0.01 SOL as well as
  the USDC
- **One half arrived and the other did not.** Each half is a separate transaction.
  The one that arrived is in the right place. Retry the other from the **Bridge** tab
  or by sending directly

If a deposit still does not appear after a few minutes, check it on a block explorer
first.

## My position will not open

**The short side is refused on Pacifica.** You have bound the agent but not deposited
into Pacifica. USDC in your Solana master is not enough on its own. See
[Deposit and bridge](/getting-started/add-funds#where-the-money-lands).

**A message about the builder fee not being approved.** Your master wallet has not
approved Kimia's fee on that exchange yet. Approve it, then try again.

**A message about size, notional limits or budget.** There is a cap of 10,000 USD per
order, and a limit on how much you can open per hour on each exchange. Reduce the
amount or wait.

**A message about leverage.** Lower it.

**A message about slippage.** The price moved too far while the order was being
placed. Try again.

**A message about market data being stale or unavailable.** Kimia refuses to price an
order against data it cannot verify. Wait a moment and try again.

**A message about residue or dust.** That asset cannot be exited cleanly within the
allowed limit. Choose a different one.

**A message about reduce only.** Opening has been paused for your account or for that
exchange. Closing still works.

**A message about rate limiting.** Too many requests in a short time. Wait a few
seconds.

**The Jupiter signing prompt was rejected or timed out.** Nothing was bought. Press
**Open position** again and approve the prompt.

See [Open your first position](/getting-started/first-position#if-the-order-is-refused)
for the full list.

## Only one side of my position opened

Kimia buys the asset before opening the short, so a partial failure normally leaves
you holding the asset rather than an unhedged short. It shows under
**Spot Holdings**.

Either sell it with **Sell**, or retry to complete the pair. Do not leave a lone short
position open, since it is exposed to the price rising.

## A close said it worked but the asset is still there

Check **Spot Holdings**. If the short closed but the spot sale did not go through,
the asset is still held. Sell it with **Sell**.

## My balance looks wrong

Positions read directly from the exchanges. If Kimia and the exchange disagree, the
exchange is right.

On Hyperliquid, money is split between spot and perp balances. Deposits and freed
margin land in spot. Kimia moves what a short needs into perp when you open.

## A tiny amount of the asset is left after closing

Expected. See [Closing a position](/using-kimia/closing#the-dust-that-gets-left-behind)
for why it happens.

## The Portfolio page shows no pairs

That list is still being wired up. Use the **Positions** list on the **Trading** page.

## The app is down

Open positions are unaffected. They live at the exchange and continue to accrue
funding whether or not Kimia is reachable.

To close while Kimia is unavailable, use the exchange's own website with your
exported master wallet key. See [Your money](/safety/your-money#getting-your-keys).
Your positions are ordinary positions and can be managed there.

## I want to stop using Kimia

Close your positions, then revoke the agent on each exchange you bound. Export your
master wallet keys and withdraw. See [Your money](/safety/your-money).

## Still stuck

Email [kimia.support@gmail.com](mailto:kimia.support@gmail.com). Include your wallet
address and the exact message you saw. Never send a private key.
