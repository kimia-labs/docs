---
title: "Glossary"
description: "Terms you will meet, in plain language."
---

**Agent**
An address Kimia creates and you bind, allowed to place orders on your Hyperliquid or
Pacifica account but not to withdraw funds.

**APR**
Annual percentage rate. A rate multiplied out to a year without compounding. Kimia's
Carry APR is the next hour's expected funding rate times 8,760.

**Auto deleveraging**
When an exchange closes somebody's profitable position to cover a loss it cannot
otherwise absorb. It can affect you even when your position is perfectly safe. See
[Risks](/safety/risks).

**Basis**
The gap between the price of an asset and the price of its perpetual future. When it
moves, a hedge that was exact becomes slightly inexact.

**Bridge**
A service that moves tokens from one blockchain to another. Kimia uses Across to move
USDC from Solana to Hyperliquid.

**Builder fee**
A fee an exchange collects on behalf of the app that placed the order. It is how
Kimia's fee is charged. See [What it costs](/using-kimia/what-it-costs).

**Carry**
Earning from holding a position over time, rather than from the price changing. Here,
the funding collected by the short. Cash and carry means buying the asset and shorting
it to collect that funding.

**Delta**
Exposure to price. A position with high delta gains and loses as the price moves.

**Delta-neutral**
Holding two positions that offset each other, so price movement affects you very
little. The strategy Kimia runs.

**Dust**
A very small leftover amount of an asset after closing, too small to sell. Usually
worth a fraction of a dollar.

**Funding rate**
A fee paid every hour between the two sides of a perpetual future, keeping its price
tethered to the real one. The source of your yield.

**Hedge**
A position taken to cancel out the risk of another one.

**Invite code**
A code, `KIM` followed by eight letters or digits, needed to join the private beta.
Whoever's code you use becomes your referrer.

**Isolated margin**
Margin that backs one position only. If it runs out, only that position is
liquidated, and the rest of your balance does not come to its rescue.

**Jupiter**
A Solana exchange that swaps tokens directly from your wallet. Kimia uses it for the
spot side only.

**Leverage**
Controlling a larger position than the money you put up. Increases both the share of
your capital that earns and the chance of liquidation.

**Liquidation**
The exchange closing your leveraged position because its margin buffer ran out. You
lose the buffer.

**Long**
A position that gains when the price rises.

**Margin**
Money set aside to back a leveraged position. The buffer that absorbs adverse price
moves.

**Master wallet**
One of two wallets, Ethereum and Solana, created for you by Privy when you sign in.
Holds your exchange accounts and your money. Signs the agent binding and fee approval
once per exchange, and every Jupiter swap.

**Notional**
The full value of a position, as opposed to the money you posted to open it. A 500
USD position at 5x leverage has a notional of 500 USD and used 100 USD of margin.

**Perpetual future, or perp**
A contract for betting on an asset's price with leverage that never expires. Kept in
line with the real price by the funding rate.

**Privy**
The sign-in provider Kimia uses. It creates and secures your master wallets until you
export their keys.

**Referral**
Someone who joined using your invite code. See [Referrals](/using-kimia/referrals).

**Short**
A position that gains when the price falls. The side that usually collects funding.

**Slippage**
The difference between the price you expected and the price you got, caused by your
own order moving the market.

**Spot**
Buying the asset itself, outright, with no leverage and no expiry.

**Stale data**
Market data old enough that Kimia will not price an order against it. A venue is
treated as stale after about 90 seconds without an update. Causes a refusal rather
than a risky order. Closing is not affected.
