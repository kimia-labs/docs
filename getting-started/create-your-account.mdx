<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with your wallet">
    Connect Phantom, MetaMask or Solflare, enter your invite code and accept the risk
    notice. See [Invite codes](/getting-started/invite-code).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kimia creates your master wallets">
    Behind the scenes, Kimia's sign-in provider, Privy, creates two wallets for you:
    an **Ethereum master** and a **Solana master**. Hyperliquid needs an Ethereum
    address and the Kimia vault needs a Solana one, which is why there are two.

    These wallets hold your money. They belong to you. Open the account menu, top
    right, to see both addresses and their balances. If your account has no Solana
    master yet, the menu and the trade ticket both offer to create one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Back up your keys">
    Use **Export key** on each master wallet in the account menu and store the keys
    somewhere safe. They are your way to your money if you ever lose access to Kimia.
    On a phone, Kimia prompts you to do this.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bind the Hyperliquid agent">
    You do this from the trade ticket, the first time you trade. When Hyperliquid is
    not yet set up, the ticket's button reads **Bind Hyperliquid agent**. Press it and
    approve the signing prompt.

    Kimia spot needs no binding. Your first Kimia trade sets up your vault instead.
    See [The Kimia vault](/using-kimia/kimia-vault).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your wallets at a glance

| Wallet | Created by | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| The wallet you sign in with | You | Proves who you are. Sends deposits, receives withdrawals |
| Ethereum master | Privy, for you | Owns your Hyperliquid account |
| Solana master | Privy, for you | Owns your Kimia vault |
| Agent | Kimia | Places and cancels orders on Hyperliquid. Holds no money |

Your sign-in wallet is not your exchange account. Money sent to Hyperliquid from
Phantom or MetaMask directly lands in a different account that Kimia cannot see.
Always use the Wallet page, or send to the master wallet addresses.

## What binding the agent allows

The agent is a separate address Kimia creates. Binding tells Hyperliquid that this
agent may trade on your Ethereum master's account.

What the agent can do:

- Open and close positions
- Cancel orders
- Change leverage and margin mode on your account
- Move USDC between your own spot and perp balances

What the agent cannot do:

- Withdraw your funds
- Send money to another address
- Approve anything else on your behalf

The line between trading and withdrawing is enforced by Hyperliquid. You can revoke
the agent at any time at Hyperliquid itself. See [Your money](/safety/your-money).

## Kimia's fee approval

Kimia's fee is collected by Hyperliquid on Kimia's behalf, which needs your Ethereum
master's one-time approval. This step is not in the app yet. When it is, you will be
asked once, before your next trade. See [What it costs](/using-kimia/what-it-costs).

## When you will be asked to sign

On Hyperliquid, the agent signs every order. After binding, opening and closing there
happens without a prompt.

For Kimia spot, your Solana master signs each buy: once per trade, sometimes twice on a
token's first trade. Closing and selling need no signature.

## If binding fails

Hyperliquid refuses to bind an agent to an empty account. If you have not deposited
yet, deposit first, then bind.

The error currently reads "Hyperliquid rejected the builder approval" for any binding
failure, including an unfunded account. If you see it, check your Hyperliquid balance
first.
