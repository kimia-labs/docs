## An invite code

Kimia is in private beta. You need an invite code from an existing user to get in.
Codes read `KIM` followed by eight letters or digits. See
[Invite codes](/getting-started/invite-code).

## A Solana wallet

Kimia signs you in with a wallet you already have. Phantom, MetaMask and Solflare all
work, but to move money in and out you need the **Solana** side of one: Phantom or
Solflare, or MetaMask with a Solana account. Deposits come from it and withdrawals go
back to it.

Signing in with only an Ethereum wallet lets you look around, but not deposit or
withdraw.

This wallet proves who you are. It is not the wallet that trades. When you sign in,
Kimia creates two new wallets for you, one on Ethereum and one on Solana, and those
hold the money. [Create your account](/getting-started/create-your-account) explains
why.

## USDC on Solana, and a little SOL

The simplest way in is USDC on Solana in the wallet you sign in with. The Wallet
page's Deposit tab sends it where it needs to go in one transaction. USDC sent to
Hyperliquid shows as Spot balance until you open a position, which moves what the
short needs into Perp. See
[Deposit and bridge](/getting-started/add-funds).

Keep some SOL too:

| Where | How much | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The wallet you sign in with | About 0.007 SOL | Network fees and rent when a deposit bridges to Hyperliquid |
| Your Solana master | At least 0.012 SOL | Only if you use Kimia as the spot venue. Checked before every Kimia trade. The first trade of a token also pays one-time rent |

## A sensible starting amount

Each side of a position must be worth at least 11 USD. At 1x leverage that is 22 USD
in total, and at 2x it is 16.50 USD.

That is the technical minimum, not a recommendation. Each trade pays exchange fees,
and the first deposit to Hyperliquid pays a one-off 1 USD account fee. At very small
sizes those costs eat most of what you earn. A few hundred dollars is a more realistic
place to start seeing the yield clearly.

## About ten minutes

Deposits usually land in under a minute. The rest is setting up.

<Note>
Expect a few signing prompts: one to deposit, one to bind the trading agent on
Hyperliquid, and one or two for your first Kimia vault trade. None of them let Kimia
send your money to anyone but you. See [Your money](/safety/your-money).
</Note>

## On your phone

Kimia works in your phone's browser at [beta.kimia.live](https://beta.kimia.live),
with a layout built for phones. See [Kimia on your phone](/using-kimia/mobile).

## What you do not need

You do not need to understand perpetual futures in detail. You do not need to watch
charts or time an entry. You do not need to keep a browser tab open, since positions
stay open whether or not you are looking at them.
