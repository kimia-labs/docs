**Effective from:** September 2026

This policy explains how Kimia ("Kimia", "we", "us") handles information when
you use the Kimia application and website.

## 1. Summary

We collect very little. There is no account signup, no password, no email required to
trade, and no identity verification. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers.

You are identified by a wallet address and an identifier from our authentication
provider, not by your name.

## 2. What we collect

**When you use the app**

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Wallet addresses: the wallet you sign in with, the master wallets created for you, and the agent address | To place orders on the correct account and show your positions |
| An account identifier from Privy, our authentication provider | To associate your wallets with your session |
| Records of orders placed through Kimia, including asset, size, status, and timestamps | To show your activity and reconcile it against the exchange |
| Snapshots of your open positions | To display them in the interface |
| Technical logs, including IP address, request times, and error details | To operate the service, diagnose faults, and prevent abuse |
| The invite code you entered, who referred you, and who you referred | To run the invite system and the referral programme |
| Trading volume, profit and loss, and referral earnings derived from your orders | To apply referral thresholds and tiers, and to rank the leaderboard |
| Records of closed pairs, including asset, sizes, prices, and funding | To show your history on the Portfolio page |
| Your Kimia vault records, held on the Solana blockchain | To trade spot for you and show your vault |

**If you join the waitlist on our website**

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your X account identifier, handle, display name, profile picture, and verified status | To sign you in with X and identify your place on the waitlist |
| A nickname, if you choose one | To show on your waitlist entry |
| Your email address, if you provide it | To contact you about access |
| A Solana wallet address | To tie your invite to one wallet |
| Your marketing preference and acknowledgements | To respect your choices |
| Your chosen pack, queue position, and timestamps | To run the waitlist |

## 3. What we do not collect

We do not collect or have access to your private keys or seed phrase.

We do not require your name, address, date of birth, government identification, or any
other identity document.

We do not run analytics, advertising, or behavioural tracking software, and we do not
sell or rent information about you to anyone.

## 4. Why we process this

We process the data above to provide the service you asked for, to keep it secure and
functioning, and to comply with legal obligations that apply to us.

Where the law requires a stated legal basis, we rely on performance of a contract for
operating the service, legitimate interests for security and fault diagnosis, and
consent for the waitlist.

## 5. Blockchain data is public and permanent

Your transactions are recorded on public blockchains and at the exchanges. That data is
visible to anyone, was never private, and cannot be deleted, altered, or recalled by
us or by you.

A wallet address is pseudonymous, not anonymous. Anyone who links an address to your
identity can see its history. Consider this before funding an address you have used
elsewhere.

**Share cards you create are public.** Anyone with the link can see the pair, its
route, prices, returns and, unless you hide amounts, dollar figures, along with your
referral code.

**The leaderboard is public.** It shows a shortened form of your wallet address with
your profit and loss, return, volume, and account value for each period.

## 6. Third parties

Kimia relies on services that receive data directly from you or from us.

| Service | Role | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Privy | Authentication and wallet infrastructure | Your login method, wallet addresses, session data |
| Hyperliquid | Exchange | Your orders, positions, and account activity |
| Jupiter | Routing Kimia vault swaps on Solana | Swap amounts and the vault accounts involved |
| Circle | Bridging USDC between Solana and Hyperliquid (CCTP) | Transfer amounts, transaction identifiers, and the addresses involved |
| X | Waitlist sign-in | Your X login, if you join the waitlist |
| Blockchain RPC providers | Reading and submitting blockchain transactions | Wallet addresses and transactions |
| Hosting and infrastructure providers | Running the service | Technical logs and traffic data |

Each operates under its own privacy policy. We do not control what they do with data
you provide to them directly.

## 7. Cookies and local storage

We do not set our own tracking or advertising cookies.

Our authentication provider stores session data in your browser, including a session
token, so that you stay signed in.

The app stores a few small entries in your browser's local storage: the invite code you
entered, the accounts admitted on this device, any referral code from a link you
opened, a record that you accepted the risk notice, and whether you chose to hide balances.
On phones, the app also remembers for the current session that it has shown the key
backup prompt. They exist so you are not asked again on every visit.

The waitlist sets its own signed session cookie while you are signed in with X.

Clearing your browser storage signs you out and may ask you to re-enter your invite
code and accept the risk notice again. It does not affect your funds or your
positions.

## 8. Retention

We keep trading and wallet records for as long as your account is active, and
afterwards where needed to meet legal, accounting, or dispute resolution obligations.

Technical logs are kept for a limited operational period and then discarded.

Waitlist entries are kept until you ask us to remove them.

## 9. Security

We limit access to production data, keep credentials out of client applications, and
enforce that our trading permissions cannot withdraw funds.

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee that data transmitted to or stored
by us will never be compromised.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the data we
hold about you, correction of it, deletion of it, a copy of it, or to object to some
processing. You can also withdraw consent for the waitlist at any time.

Contact us at the address below and we will respond within the period the applicable
law requires.

Two limits are worth stating plainly. We cannot delete data recorded on a public
blockchain or held by an exchange, because we do not control it. We may need to retain
certain records to meet legal obligations even after a deletion request.

## 11. Children

Kimia is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from
children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete
it.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the effective date
above and, where practical, announced in the interface.
