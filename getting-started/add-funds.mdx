---

Your money goes into your own account at the exchange. Kimia never holds it and
never takes possession of it.

Each exchange is funded separately. There is no transfer between them.

## Hyperliquid

Hyperliquid accepts USDC. Deposit through Hyperliquid's own interface, using the same
wallet you signed in to Kimia with.

Once the deposit confirms, your balance appears in Kimia automatically. Nothing needs
connecting or importing.

<Note>
Deposit before approving the agent on Hyperliquid. The exchange refuses agent
approvals on an empty account.
</Note>

## Pacifica

Pacifica runs on Solana and accepts USDC on Solana.

<Warning>
The Pacifica deposit screen is not yet part of the main Kimia app. For now, deposit
through Pacifica directly, using the Solana address shown in your Kimia account.

Approving the agent is not enough on its own. Without a deposit, the short side of
your position will be refused by the exchange, and the position will not open.
</Warning>

## Which one to fund

If you are starting out, pick one and fund only that one. A position that lives
entirely on a single exchange is simpler to reason about and cheaper to close.

Funding both opens up combinations where you buy on one exchange and short on the
other, which is sometimes the better rate. It also means keeping enough spare balance
on both sides, since Kimia cannot move money between them to cover a shortfall.

## How much to leave spare

Do not put every last dollar into a position.

The short side needs a margin buffer. If the price rises sharply, that buffer absorbs
the move. If it runs out, the exchange closes the position for you at a loss, which is
called liquidation, and your hedge disappears at the moment you most need it.

Keeping some unused balance on the exchange gives you room to top up rather than
being forced out. See [Risks](/safety/risks).

## Withdrawing

You withdraw through the exchange, the same way you deposited. Kimia is not involved
and cannot block it.

Close any open position first. Funds backing a live position are not available to
withdraw.
