<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an asset">
    The main screen lists assets with the funding rate each exchange is paying right
    now, shown as an annualized percentage.

    A higher rate is not automatically better. Rates on smaller assets swing harder
    and reverse more often. Bitcoin and Ethereum pay less and behave more predictably.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose how much">
    Enter the amount you want to commit. Kimia splits it between the two sides for
    you.

    Each side must clear 10 USD, so very small amounts will be rejected.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose leverage">
    This decides how much of your money earns and how much sits as a safety buffer.

    Start at 1x or 2x. The extra yield above 3x is small and the added fragility is
    not.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Kimia checks the order against its safety limits, then places both sides. No
    wallet signature is needed, because you already approved the agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens when you confirm

Kimia buys the asset first, then opens the short. The order matters. If the second
step fails for any reason, you are left holding the asset, which is a position you
understand and can sell.

Doing it the other way round could leave you holding only a short position, which
loses money if the price rises. Kimia does not do that.

## Reading the confirmation

Once both sides are open you will see them in your positions list. Two entries, one
long and one short, roughly equal in size.

They will not be exactly equal. Prices move between the two orders, and each exchange
rounds sizes differently. A small mismatch is normal and is the reason your returns
will not track the funding rate perfectly.

## If the order is refused

Kimia checks every order before placing it and will refuse rather than proceed if
something is wrong. Common reasons:

| Message mentions | What to do |
|---|---|
| Notional or size limit | Reduce the amount. There is a cap per position |
| Leverage limit | Lower the leverage |
| Market not available | That asset is not currently tradeable through Kimia |
| Stale or unavailable market data | Wait a moment and try again |
| Residue or dust | Pick a different asset. This one cannot be exited cleanly |

A refusal is the system working. It means the order was understood and judged unsafe,
not that something broke. See [Troubleshooting](/safety/troubleshooting) if you see
something else.

## After it opens

Funding is paid hourly by the exchange, directly into the account holding the short
position. You do not need to claim it.

There is nothing to do while a position is open. It stays open whether or not you are
signed in.
