## The approval signature failed

**On Hyperliquid, with a message about an unfunded account.** The exchange requires a
balance before it accepts an agent. Deposit first, then approve.

**With a message that the wallet is already approved.** It worked already, possibly on
an earlier attempt. Nothing further is needed and no signature was wasted.

**With a message about the master address.** Your account is bound to a different
wallet than the one you are signed in with. Sign in with the original wallet.

## My position will not open

**The short side is refused on Pacifica.** You have approved the agent but not
deposited. Approval alone is not enough. See [Add funds](/getting-started/add-funds).

**A message about size or notional limits.** There is a cap per position. Reduce the
amount.

**A message about leverage.** Lower it.

**A message about market data being stale or unavailable.** Kimia refuses to price an
order against data it cannot verify. Wait a moment and try again.

**A message about residue or dust.** That asset cannot be exited cleanly within the
allowed limit. Choose a different one.

## Only one side of my position opened

Kimia buys the asset before opening the short, so a partial failure normally leaves
you holding the asset rather than an unhedged short.

Either close the remaining side, or retry to complete the pair. Do not leave a lone
short position open, since it is exposed to the price rising.

## My balance looks wrong

Positions read directly from the exchange. If Kimia and the exchange disagree, the
exchange is right.

Deposits can take a few minutes to confirm. If a deposit does not appear after that,
check it on the exchange first, since the problem is more likely there than in Kimia.

## A tiny amount of the asset is left after closing

Expected. See [Closing a position](/using-kimia/closing) for why it happens and why it
cannot be avoided.

## The app is down

Open positions are unaffected. They live at the exchange and continue to accrue
funding whether or not Kimia is reachable.

To close while Kimia is unavailable, use the exchange's own interface directly. Your
positions are ordinary positions and can be managed there.

## I want to stop using Kimia

Close your positions, then revoke the agent on each exchange you approved. See
[Your money](/safety/your-money).
