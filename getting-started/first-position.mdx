---
title: "Open your first position"
description: "Choosing an asset, venues, a size and a leverage."
---

Positions are opened from the trade ticket on the **Trading** page. The ticket has
two tabs. **Cash & carry** is the strategy these docs describe: buy the asset, short
it as a perp. **Dual perp** is not available yet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an asset">
    The asset picker lists every asset Kimia can pair, with its price, the spread
    between spot and perp, and its **Carry APR**. You can sort by any of them or
    search by name.

    Carry APR is the funding rate the best short venue is expected to pay over the
    next hour, multiplied out to a year. It is gross, before any fees. A higher rate
    is not automatically better: rates on smaller assets swing harder and reverse
    more often. Bitcoin and Ethereum pay less and behave more predictably.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the venues">
    **Spot venue** is where the asset is bought: Hyperliquid, Pacifica or Jupiter.
    **Short venue** is where the perp is sold: Hyperliquid or Pacifica. The venue
    Kimia recommends is marked with a dot.

    Using the same exchange for both sides is simplest. Different venues can pay
    more, but each side is funded separately.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Type how much USDC to commit in **Total USDC**, or press **Max**. Kimia splits it
    between the two sides for you.

    Each side must be worth at least 10 USD. At 1x that means 20 USD in total, and at
    2x it means 15 USD.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose leverage">
    The slider runs from 1x up to the maximum the asset allows. It starts at 2x.

    Leverage decides how much of your money earns and how much sits as a safety
    buffer on the short. Start at 1x or 2x. The extra yield above 3x is small and the
    added fragility is not.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open">
    The summary under the ticket shows the size of each side, the margin posted, the
    net exposure and the carry at the current rate. Check it, then press
    **Open position**.

    If an exchange is not set up yet, the button reads **Bind Hyperliquid agent** or
    **Bind Pacifica agent** instead. See
    [Create your account](/getting-started/create-your-account).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
There is no separate confirmation screen. Pressing **Open position** places the
orders. The summary is your last look.
</Note>

## Signatures

With Hyperliquid or Pacifica on both sides, no signing prompt appears. The agent you
bound signs the orders.

If Jupiter is the spot venue, your Solana master signs the purchase. You will see one
prompt, showing the swap and its fee.

## What happens when you press Open

Kimia buys the asset first, then opens the short. The order matters. If the second
step fails, you are left holding the asset, which is a position you understand and
can sell.

Doing it the other way round could leave you holding only a short position, which
loses money if the price rises. Kimia does not do that.

Opening also changes two settings on that asset for your exchange account: it sets
the margin mode to isolated, and sets the leverage you chose. If you also trade that
account by hand, be aware of this.

## Reading the result

The position appears as one row in the **Positions** list at the bottom of the page,
showing both sides together.

The two sides will not be exactly equal. Prices move between the two orders, and each
exchange rounds sizes differently. A small mismatch is normal.

## If the ticket will not let you open

The ticket checks a few things before the button works:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| Each leg needs at least $10 notional | Increase the amount, or lower the leverage |
| That is more than the $X these venues can fund | Reduce the amount, or deposit more |
| \{Asset\} allows at most Nx | Lower the leverage |
| \{Venue\} does not list \{Asset\} spot | Pick one of the spot venues it names |

A warning that spot is some percent above or below the perp does not block you, but
read it. That gap is a cost on entry.

## If the order is refused

After you press Open, Kimia checks the order again and refuses rather than proceed if
something is wrong. Common reasons:

| Refusal mentions | What to do |
|---|---|
| Notional or size limit | Reduce the amount. The cap is 10,000 USD per order |
| Notional budget | You have opened a lot in the last hour. Wait and try again |
| Leverage limit | Lower the leverage. The cap is 20x, or less if the asset allows less |
| Market not allowed | That asset is not currently tradeable through Kimia |
| Slippage | The price moved too far while placing. Try again |
| Stale or unavailable market data | Wait a moment and try again |
| Residue or dust | Pick a different asset. This one cannot be exited cleanly |
| Reduce only | Opening is paused for your account or that exchange. Closing still works |
| Builder fee not approved | Approve Kimia's fee on that exchange, then try again |

A refusal is the system working. It means the order was understood and judged unsafe,
not that something broke. See [Troubleshooting](/safety/troubleshooting).

## After it opens

Funding is paid by the exchange every hour, directly into the account holding the
short. You do not need to claim it. The positions list shows a countdown to the next
payment.

There is nothing to do while a position is open. It stays open whether or not you are
signed in. Kimia does not rebalance it or top up its margin for you.
