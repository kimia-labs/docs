## Kimia does not hold your funds

Your money sits in your own account at Hyperliquid or Pacifica. Kimia has no account
holding customer deposits and no ability to move your funds to itself or anywhere
else.

You can withdraw at any time, through the exchange, without Kimia's involvement or
permission.

## The two addresses

When you sign in, two addresses are created for you.

**Your main address** holds the exchange account and the money. It is yours. It signs
exactly one thing, once per exchange, during setup.

**The agent address** is created by Kimia and is allowed to place orders on your
account. It holds no money of its own.

The exchange enforces the difference. The agent's permission covers trading only. It
is not a permission the software grants itself or can widen later.

## What the agent can and cannot do

| Can | Cannot |
|---|---|
| Open positions | Withdraw funds |
| Close positions | Transfer to another address |
| Cancel orders | Approve further permissions |
| Change leverage on your account | Access your main wallet |

The worst case if the agent were misused is unwanted trading on your account, which
could lose money. It is not the same as your funds being taken, and the distinction is
enforced by the exchange rather than by trust.

## Revoking access

You can revoke the agent at any time through the exchange's own interface, where
approved agents are listed. Revoking there takes effect immediately and does not
require Kimia to cooperate.

After revoking:

- Open positions stay open. Revoking does not close anything
- Kimia can no longer place or close orders for you
- You manage or close remaining positions through the exchange directly

Revoke if you stop using Kimia, or at any point you are uncomfortable.

## If something looks wrong

Trades you did not initiate, or a position you do not recognise, warrant acting first
and asking afterwards.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Revoke the agent at the exchange">
    This stops any further orders immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close open positions through the exchange">
    You do not need Kimia to do this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw if you want to">
    Your main wallet retains full control throughout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two exchanges, two separate accounts

Approving Hyperliquid does not approve Pacifica, and revoking one does not revoke the
other. Each is separate, with separate funds and a separate permission.

If you decide to stop using Kimia entirely, revoke on both.
