Every Kimia account has its own invite code. Because Kimia is in private beta, your
code is also how other people get in.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share your invite link">
    On the **Leaderboard** page, press **Share Referral URL**. Copy the link or post
    it to X. The link opens a page showing your code and a **Start trading** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Both of you trade">
    A referral only counts once they have traded 5,000 USD in volume. You earn
    nothing until you have traded 10,000 USD yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You earn a share of their fees">
    You receive a share of the Kimia fees your active referrals generate. The share
    rises with how many active referrals you hold.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tiers

| Tier | Active referrals | Share of their Kimia fees |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 0 to 4 | 5 percent |
| Builder | 5 to 19 | 15 percent |
| Pro | 20 to 49 | 25 percent |
| Elite | 50 or more | 35 percent |

An active referral is one that has passed the 5,000 USD volume threshold. Your current
tier, number of referrals and earnings show in the overview panel on the
**Leaderboard** page.

The share comes out of Kimia's own fee. It does not add anything to what your
referrals pay. See [What it costs](/using-kimia/what-it-costs).

## Rules

- Someone becomes your referral by entering your code within 7 days of creating
  their account. See [Invite codes](/getting-started/invite-code)
- A referrer cannot be changed once set
- You cannot refer yourself, and referrals cannot loop back to someone who referred you
- Only genuine trading counts. Wash trading and multiple accounts run by one person
  are excluded, and rewards can be withheld from activity found to be abusive

## Good to know

Earnings are tracked as they accrue. How and when they are paid out has not been
announced yet, and neither has any reward for the person being referred. Both will be
stated here when decided.

Treat the figures on this page as a description of the programme today. It may change
during the beta.
