Every day, traders using leverage pay a fee to hold their positions open. That fee is
called the funding rate, and it usually flows from people betting the price will rise
to people betting it will fall.

Kimia lets you collect that fee without taking a view on the price yourself.

## The short version

You put up money. Kimia opens two positions at the same time that cancel each other
out: one that profits if the price rises, and one that profits by the same amount if
the price falls. Your exposure to the price is close to zero.

What you keep is the funding rate.

## What this is not

Kimia is not staking. It is not a savings account, and nothing about the return is
fixed or guaranteed. It is not a points programme or an airdrop farm.

The yield comes from an actual fee paid by actual traders. When fewer people want
leverage, the fee shrinks. Sometimes it goes negative, and the position costs you
money instead of paying you.

## Where your money lives

Kimia does not hold your funds. You deposit directly into your own account at one of
two exchanges, Hyperliquid and Pacifica, and you can withdraw from those accounts at
any time without asking Kimia for permission.

What you grant Kimia is a trading permission, not custody. See
[Your money](/safety/your-money) for exactly what that permission allows and how to
revoke it.

<Warning>
Kimia is early software handling real money on live exchanges. Start with an amount
you are willing to lose entirely while you learn how it behaves.
</Warning>
