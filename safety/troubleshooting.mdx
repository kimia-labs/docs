## I cannot get past the invite screen

See [Invite codes](/getting-started/invite-code#if-your-code-is-refused) for each
message and what it means.

## Binding the Hyperliquid agent failed

**"Hyperliquid rejected the builder approval".** Despite the wording, this appears for
any binding failure. The most common cause is an empty account: Hyperliquid refuses
agents on unfunded accounts. Deposit to HyperCore first, then bind.

**The wallet is already approved.** It worked already. Nothing further is needed.

**The address is already bound to another account.** That master wallet is linked to a
different Kimia account. Sign in with the wallet you used originally.

## I cannot deposit or withdraw

**"Connect the Solana wallet holding your USDC".** Deposits and withdrawals use the
Solana side of your sign-in wallet. If you signed in with an Ethereum-only wallet, sign
in again with Phantom, Solflare, or MetaMask with a Solana account.

**Refused before signing.** The amount going to HyperCore is too small to cover the
bridge fee, plus 1 USD on your first HyperCore deposit.

**Failed while signing.** Check your sign-in wallet holds about 0.007 SOL as well as
the USDC.

**"Not filled yet".** The bridge is still working. Follow the transaction link before
trying again.

**"The bridge did not reach HyperCore".** Email support with the transaction link
shown.

**Withdrawal from HyperCore is short.** 0.50 USD stays behind for Hyperliquid's gas,
and Circle's fee comes out on arrival. While a position is open, only spot balance can
be withdrawn.

**It has not arrived.** HyperCore withdrawals can take a few minutes. Press
**Refresh balances** in the account menu, and check again before retrying.

## Kimia vault

**"Add SOL for fees".** Your Solana master needs at least 0.012 SOL. Send SOL to its
address from the account menu.

**"Create Solana wallet".** Your account has no Solana master yet. Press the button.

**"Kimia vault unavailable".** Kimia cannot read your vault right now. Wait, or choose
Hyperliquid spot.

**"Your authorization for this token has expired".** Open the **Vault** tab and press
**Reauthorize** on that token.

**"Kimia's trading key changed".** Authorize the token again on the **Vault** tab.

**"The Kimia vault is paused".** Kimia has paused buys. Nothing was traded. Withdrawals
still work.

**"The buy expired before it landed".** You took a little long to sign. Kimia prepares
a fresh one automatically; approve it.

**"Sold, but the USDC is not in the vault yet"** after **Withdraw as USDC**. The USDC
from the sale usually goes straight to your Solana master. Check its balance before
doing anything else.

## My position will not open

See [Open your first position](/getting-started/first-position#if-the-order-is-refused)
for each refusal and what to do. The common ones:

**Size, notional limits or budget.** There is a cap of 10,000 USD per order, and a
limit on how much you can open per hour. Reduce the amount or wait.

**Builder approved for less than this order charges.** Kimia's fee approval is missing.
The approval step is not in the app yet. Email support.

**Stale or unavailable market data.** Wait a moment and try again.

**Reduce only.** Opening has been paused. Closing still works.

**Rate limiting.** Too many requests in a short time. Wait a few seconds.

## The order timed out

It may still land. Check your positions first. Retrying from the same ticket replays
the same order rather than opening a second one.

## Only the spot side opened

Kimia shows "Short failed. Spot is open and unhedged." The spot appears under
**Spot Holdings**, not **Positions**. Sell it with **Sell**, or open again.

## A close only partly worked

If Kimia says "partly closed, spot still held", check **Spot Holdings** and sell what
is there. If it is empty, the pair is closed and only a follow-up step, such as moving
margin back to spot, did not finish.

## Prices or rates show a dash

A dash means Kimia has no fresh rate for that venue, usually because the data is more
than about 90 seconds old. It clears by itself. Kimia shows a dash rather than an old
number.

If prices pause briefly on a shared network, Kimia reconnects automatically.

## My balance looks wrong

Press **Refresh balances** in the account menu. If Kimia and Hyperliquid disagree,
Hyperliquid is right.

On Hyperliquid, money is split between spot and perp balances. Deposits and freed
margin land in spot. Kimia moves what a short needs into perp when you open.

## A tiny amount of the asset is left after closing

Expected. See [Closing a position](/using-kimia/closing#the-dust-that-gets-left-behind).

## A closed pair is missing from Portfolio

Closed pairs appear after their fills are confirmed, which can take a few minutes.
Pairs closed before 28 October 2026 are not recorded.

## The app is down

Open positions are unaffected. They continue to accrue funding whether or not Kimia is
reachable.

To act while Kimia is unavailable, use your exported keys: the Ethereum master at
Hyperliquid, the Solana master to withdraw from your vault. See
[Your money](/safety/your-money#getting-your-keys).

## I want to stop using Kimia

Close your positions, withdraw everything, revoke your vault authorizations on the
Vault tab, and revoke the Hyperliquid agent. See [Your money](/safety/your-money).

## Still stuck

Email [kimia.support@gmail.com](mailto:kimia.support@gmail.com). Include your wallet
address, the exact message you saw, and any transaction link. Never send a private
key.
