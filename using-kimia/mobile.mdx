title: "Kimia on your phone"
description: "The phone layout, and where everything lives."
---

Open [beta.kimia.live](https://beta.kimia.live) in your phone's browser. Phones get a
layout built for them automatically. There is no app to install. Tablets get the
desktop layout.

It is the same account, the same positions and the same money as on a computer.

## Getting in

The same steps as on a computer: **Connect Wallet**, enter your invite code, accept
the risk notice. See [Invite codes](/getting-started/invite-code).

Then Kimia asks you to **Keep your funds safe** by saving your wallet keys. **Back up
now** exports your Ethereum key, for your Hyperliquid account, and your Solana key, for
your Solana wallet and vault. **Skip for now** asks again on your next visit. Backing
up is worth doing: these keys are your way to your money without Kimia.

## The screens

| Screen | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Wallet** | Your balances and the **+** menu for moving money. The home screen |
| **Portfolio** | Your pairs as cards |
| **Activity** | Closed pairs, each shareable |
| **Leaderboard** | Rankings and your referral standing |
| **Settings** | Profile and key export, referrals, docs, terms, support, log out |

## Moving money

The **+** on the Wallet screen offers **Receive**, **Deposit**, **Withdraw**,
**Bridge** and **Vault**. They work exactly as on the computer's Wallet page. See
[Deposit and bridge](/getting-started/add-funds) and [Withdraw](/getting-started/withdraw).

Balances show your **Solana wallet**, **HyperCore spot**, **HyperCore perp** and
**Kimia vault**.

## Trading

Trading opens as a sheet over the screen. It is the same trade ticket as on a
computer, with the asset's carry, spread, spot and short venues, maximum leverage and
mark price above it. **Change pair** lets you search, filter and sort assets.

Positions show as cards with funding, earned, unrealized profit and loss, and what the
pair is earning per day. **Close** works the same way.

## Sharing on a phone

The share sheet has **Hide amounts**, which removes dollar figures from the card and
its link before you post.

## Good to know

**Close all pairs** is only on the computer layout's Portfolio page for now.

[kimia.support@gmail.com](mailto:kimia.support@gmail.com) instead.
