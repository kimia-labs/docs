---
title: "Open your first position"
description: "Choosing an asset, a spot venue, a size and a leverage."
---

Positions are opened from the trade ticket on the **Trading** page. The ticket has
two tabs. **Cash & carry** is the strategy these docs describe: buy the asset, short
it as a perp. **Dual perp** is not available yet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an asset">
    The asset picker lists every asset with a Hyperliquid perp and spot on Hyperliquid
    or Kimia. Columns show the price, the spot price on Hyperliquid and on Kimia, the
    spread between spot and perp, and the funding rate, shown as **HL funding** and
    again as **Carry APR**. Sort by price, spread or Carry APR, filter by spot venue,
    or search by name.

    Carry APR is the funding rate Hyperliquid is expected to pay over the next hour,
    multiplied out to a year. It is gross, before any fees. A higher rate is not
    automatically better: rates on smaller assets swing harder and reverse more often.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the spot venue">
    **Spot venue** is where the asset is bought: **Hyperliquid** or **Kimia**. Kimia
    buys inside the Kimia vault on Solana. See [The Kimia vault](/using-kimia/kimia-vault).
    The venue Kimia recommends is marked with a dot. A venue that does not list the
    asset is greyed out.

    **Short venue** is always Hyperliquid.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Type how much USDC to commit in **Total USDC**, or press **Max**. Kimia splits it
    between the two sides for you.

    Each side must be worth at least 11 USD. At 1x that means 22 USD in total, and at
    2x it means 16.50 USD.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose leverage">
    The slider runs from 1x up to the maximum the asset allows. It starts at 2x.

    Leverage decides how much of your money earns and how much sits as a safety
    buffer on the short. Start at 1x or 2x. The extra yield above 3x is small and the
    added fragility is not.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open">
    The summary under the ticket shows each side's size, the margin posted, the net
    exposure and the carry per day at the current rate. Check it, then press
    **Open position**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
There is no separate confirmation screen. Pressing **Open position** places the
orders. The summary is your last look.
</Note>

## What the button might say instead

| Button | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Connect wallet** | Sign in |
| **Bind Hyperliquid agent** | Approve the agent once. See [Create your account](/getting-started/create-your-account) |
| **Create Solana wallet** | Kimia spot needs a Solana master. Press it to create one |
| **Add SOL for fees** | Send at least 0.012 SOL to your Solana master |
| **Reading vault…**, **Kimia vault unavailable** | Wait, or pick Hyperliquid spot |

## Signatures

With Hyperliquid spot, no prompt appears. The agent you bound signs both orders.

With Kimia spot, your Solana master signs the buy: one prompt, or two on the first
trade of a token. If the signature takes too long, Kimia prepares a fresh one
automatically.

## What happens when you press Open

Kimia buys the spot first, then opens the short, sized to match the amount of spot
that actually arrived. If the spot only partly fills, the short is made smaller to
match.

The order matters. If the short then fails, you hold only spot, which you can sell,
never a lone short that loses money as the price rises. Kimia tells you plainly:
"Short failed. Spot is open and unhedged." Sell it from **Spot Holdings**, or try
again.

Opening also sets your margin mode to isolated and the leverage you chose for that
asset on your Hyperliquid account. If you also trade that account by hand, be aware
of this.

## If the order timed out

If the exchange does not confirm in time, Kimia says the order "may still land".
Check your positions before doing anything. Retrying from the same ticket replays the
same order rather than opening a second one.

## If the ticket will not let you open

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| Each leg needs at least $11 notional | Increase the amount, or lower the leverage |
| That is more than the $X these venues can fund | Reduce the amount, or deposit more |
| \{Asset\} allows at most Nx on Hyperliquid | Lower the leverage |
| \{Venue\} has no \{Asset\} spot | Pick the spot venue it suggests |

A warning that spot is some percent above or below the perp does not block you, but
read it. That gap is a cost on entry.

## If the order is refused

After you press Open, Kimia checks the order again and refuses rather than proceed if
something is wrong:

| Refusal mentions | What to do |
|---|---|
| Notional or size limit | Reduce the amount. The cap is 10,000 USD per order |
| Notional budget | You have opened a lot in the last hour. Wait and try again |
| Leverage limit | Lower the leverage. The cap is 20x, or less if the asset allows less |
| Market not allowed | That asset is not currently tradeable through Kimia |
| Slippage | The price moved too far while placing. Try again |
| Stale or unavailable market data | Wait a moment and try again |
| Residue or dust | Pick a different asset. This one cannot be exited cleanly |
| Reduce only | Opening is paused for your account or for Hyperliquid. Closing still works |
| Builder approved for less than this order charges | Kimia's fee approval is missing. See [What it costs](/using-kimia/what-it-costs) |
| Under the $10 minimum | A leg would be below the venue's minimum, usually the short after a Kimia swap. Raise the size |
| Probably a different token | Kimia spot only: the swap price is more than 5 percent from the perp. Pick Hyperliquid spot |
| Vault paused, authorization expired | See [Troubleshooting](/safety/troubleshooting#kimia-vault) |

A refusal is the system working. It means the order was understood and judged unsafe,
not that something broke.

## After it opens

Funding is paid by Hyperliquid every hour, directly into the account holding the
short. You do not need to claim it. The positions list shows a countdown to the next
payment.

There is nothing to do while a position is open. It stays open whether or not you are
signed in.
