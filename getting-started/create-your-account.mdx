<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with your wallet">
    Connect Phantom, MetaMask or Solflare, enter your invite code and accept the risk
    notice. See [Invite codes](/getting-started/invite-code).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kimia creates your master wallets">
    Behind the scenes, Kimia's sign-in provider, Privy, creates two wallets for you:
    an **Ethereum master** and a **Solana master**. The two exchanges need different
    kinds of address, which is why there are two.

    These are the wallets that hold your exchange accounts and your money. They
    belong to you. Open the account menu, top right, to see both addresses and their
    balances.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bind an agent on each exchange">
    You do this from the trade ticket, the first time you trade. When an exchange is
    not yet set up, the ticket's button reads **Bind Hyperliquid agent** or
    **Bind Pacifica agent**. Press it and approve the signing prompt.

    A position that uses both exchanges needs both bound, one at a time. Jupiter has
    no agent of its own. It needs Pacifica bound, because both use your Solana master.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve Kimia's fee on each exchange">
    Each exchange also asks your master wallet to approve Kimia's fee once. The
    approval sets a ceiling of 0.1 percent. Kimia currently charges 0.05 percent on
    the short side only. See [What it costs](/using-kimia/what-it-costs).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your wallets at a glance

| Wallet | Created by | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| The wallet you sign in with | You | Proves who you are. Pays for in-app deposits |
| Ethereum master | Privy, for you | Holds your Hyperliquid account |
| Solana master | Privy, for you | Holds your Pacifica account and any Jupiter spot holdings |
| Agent | Kimia | Places and cancels orders on Hyperliquid and Pacifica. Holds no money |

Your sign-in wallet is not your exchange account. Money sent to Hyperliquid or
Pacifica from Phantom or MetaMask directly lands in a different account that Kimia
cannot see. Always fund the master wallets, or use the in-app deposit.

## What binding the agent allows

The agent is a separate address Kimia creates. Binding tells the exchange that this
agent may trade on your master wallet's account.

What the agent can do:

- Open and close positions
- Cancel orders
- Change leverage and margin mode on your account
- On Hyperliquid, move USDC between your own spot and perp balances

What the agent cannot do:

- Withdraw your funds
- Send money to another address
- Approve anything else on your behalf

The line between trading and withdrawing is enforced by the exchange. You can revoke
the agent at any time. See [Your money](/safety/your-money).

## When you will be asked to sign again

On Hyperliquid and Pacifica, the agent signs every order. After binding and the fee
approval, opening and closing there happens without a prompt.

Jupiter is different. Solana has no way to delegate trading, so your Solana master
signs every Jupiter purchase and every Jupiter sale. Expect one prompt per trade when
Jupiter is one side of your position.

## If Hyperliquid rejects the binding

Hyperliquid refuses to bind an agent to an empty account. If you have not deposited
yet, binding fails with a message about the account being unfunded.

Deposit first, then bind. Pacifica lets you bind before depositing, but a Pacifica
order still needs funds on the exchange.
