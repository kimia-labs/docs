Both pages are in the top navigation, next to **Trading**.

## Portfolio

The **Portfolio** page summarises your account over time. Switch between **All**,
**Orders** and **PnL report** at the top.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| PnL | Profit and loss over time |
| Analytics | Wallet balance, average buy amount, average hold time, biggest win and biggest loss |
| Distribution | How your results are spread across outcomes |

### Your pairs

Below the cards, **Open Pairs** and **Closed Pairs** list your positions pair by pair,
with entry price, funding, earned and unrealized profit and loss. Filters narrow the
list to pairs that are earning or paying funding, in profit or at a loss, or above
1,000 or 10,000 USD.

<Note>
The pairs list on this page is still being wired up and may show as empty even when
you hold positions. Use the **Positions** list on the **Trading** page, which reads
live from the exchanges.
</Note>

### Sharing a pair

**Share this pair** creates a card for that pair, which you can copy as a link or
post to X. Check what it shows before posting.

## Leaderboard

The **Leaderboard** page ranks Kimia users. Choose a period: **1 Day**, **1 Week**,
**1 Month** or **All Time**.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Place | Rank for the period |
| User | A shortened wallet address |
| PnL | Profit and loss over the period |
| ROI | Return on the money committed |
| Volume | Total traded over the period |
| Account Value | Current account size |

Your own standing appears in the overview panel alongside your referral details:
**Referrals**, **Referral Tier** and **Referral Earnings**. **Total Points** is shown
but points are not active yet.

<Note>
The leaderboard is public. Anyone can see a shortened form of your wallet address and
your figures for each period. Nothing else about you is shown.
</Note>

For referrals, see [Referrals](/using-kimia/referrals).
