Your money goes into your own master wallets. Kimia never takes possession of it.

Moving money happens on the **Wallet** page, in the top navigation. It has four tabs:
**Deposit**, **Withdraw**, **Bridge** and **Vault**.

## Where money needs to be

| For | Money needs to be | Arrives through |
|---|---|---|
| A Hyperliquid short, or Hyperliquid spot | USDC on HyperCore, in your Ethereum master's account | Deposit or Bridge |
| Kimia spot | USDC in your Solana master or your vault | Deposit |

## The Deposit tab

Fund both places in one transaction, from the Solana wallet you signed in with.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter two amounts">
    **To your Solana wallet** goes to your Solana master, for Kimia spot.
    **To HyperCore** is bridged to your Hyperliquid account. Either can be zero. Each
    has a **Max** that fills whatever the other leaves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Deposit and approve once">
    One wallet prompt covers both. The HyperCore figure then changes from what you
    typed to what the bridge quote says will land, after its fee. To see that quote
    before committing, use the Bridge tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it arrive">
    Both legs go in one Solana transaction, so if it fails, neither moves. The
    HyperCore share then waits for Circle to forward it, which can occasionally fail.
    The app shows each leg as it progresses, with a link to the transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The bridge to HyperCore is Circle's CCTP, the official USDC bridge. It usually lands
in under a minute.

## Fees and limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Bridge fee | About 0.25 USD, near-flat whatever the size. The quote is authoritative |
| First deposit to HyperCore | 1 USD extra, a one-off fee to open your HyperCore account |
| Minimum to HyperCore | More than the fee, plus the 1 USD on a first deposit |
| Suggested minimum | 20 USD. Below that the app warns you |
| SOL needed | About 0.007 SOL in your sign-in wallet when bridging. About 0.004 SOL of it is rent for the bridge message, not returned |
| Maximum | 1,000,000 USD per leg |

## The Bridge tab

Moves USDC between Solana and HyperCore in either direction. Use the arrow to switch.

- **Solana to HyperCore** sends from your sign-in wallet to your Hyperliquid account.
  Same fees and SOL rule as Deposit. The quote shows rate, fee, slippage and the
  minimum you will receive
- **HyperCore to Solana** sends from your Hyperliquid account to your sign-in wallet.
  Minimum 2 USD. Circle takes its fee on arrival. This is the same route as
  [Withdraw](/getting-started/withdraw)

## Sending directly

The account menu, top right, shows each master wallet with a **Deposit** button that
reveals its address and a QR code.

- **Ethereum master.** Send only USDC on HyperCore.
- **Solana master.** USDC on Solana. It also accepts SOL, which it needs for Kimia
  vault fees, even though the card only mentions USDC.

Sending any other token or network risks losing it.

## Where the money lands

**Hyperliquid.** Bridged USDC arrives in your HyperCore spot balance. When you open a
position, Kimia moves whatever the short needs from spot to perp for you.

**Solana master.** USDC waits there. Each Kimia buy pulls in whatever it needs. You can also move it in yourself from the Vault tab.

Use **Refresh balances** in the account menu if a balance looks out of date.

## How much to leave spare

Do not put every last dollar into a position.

The short is backed by margin held against that position alone, called isolated
margin. If the price rises sharply, that margin absorbs the move. If it runs out, the
exchange closes the short, which is called liquidation. Kimia then sells your spot
automatically, or for Kimia spot it cannot sell returns the tokens to
your Solana master, so you are not left holding it unhedged. You lose the margin. See
[Risks](/safety/risks).

Spare balance on Hyperliquid does not protect the short by itself. It gives you funds
to add to the position's margin at the exchange if you need to.
